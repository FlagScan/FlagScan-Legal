Privacy Policy

Flag Scan · Effective October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Seismic Systems Inc. (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Flag Scan mobile application (the “App”). It also explains the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the App.

Summary

  • The photos you scan are never uploaded to our servers. Flag identification runs entirely on your device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address and a sign-in credential, plus references to your recent scans.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. You can also choose to watch optional ads to earn extra scans.
  • Flag Scan Pro is a paid subscription. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, and subscription status is managed by RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. We may also receive a display name if you provide one or if your sign-in method supplies it. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth, which stores them only in hashed (one-way encrypted) form. We cannot see or recover your password.

Support communications. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

1.2 Photos and camera

The App can use your device camera and photo library, but only if you grant permission in the App or in your device settings. When you scan an image, it is analyzed by an image classification model that runs locally on your device to identify the flag. The image itself is not uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on our servers or any server we control.

To power the “recent scans” feature, we store a reference (a link or identifier) that points to the photo in your device’s photo library. We do not store a copy of the photo. If you delete or move the original photo on your device, the reference may stop working. You can turn camera and photo permissions off at any time, and doing so does not delete your account.

1.3 Subscription and purchase information

If you subscribe to Flag Scan Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play, which collect and hold your payment details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to verify purchases and determine whether your Pro entitlement is active. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information, such as the product you purchased, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status, and an app user identifier. We do not receive or store your credit card number or other payment instrument details.

1.4 Advertising and device information

The free version of the App shows banner and small display ads, and optional rewarded ads that you can watch to earn additional scans. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating system details, app interactions, and ad performance data to deliver, personalize, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising. Subscribers to Flag Scan Pro do not see ads. You can learn more in Google’s explanation of how it uses data from apps that use its services and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

1.5 Information stored on your device

The App uses your device’s local storage (AsyncStorage) to save data such as your recent scans, your display name, your settings (for example dark mode and permission toggles), a cached copy of basic account details, and your Pro subscription status. We do this to make the App load faster, avoid visual flicker, and allow certain features to work offline. This data remains on your device. It is removed when you sign out, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App.

1.6 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, or the images you scan. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information.

3. Pro offline access

Flag Scan Pro can continue to work without an internet connection for up to three (3) days after the App last verified your subscription. To make this possible, your Pro status and the time of the last check are stored on your device. After three days without a connection, Pro features are disabled until the App reconnects to our servers and verifies your subscription again.

  • Legitimate interests: to keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve reliability, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law.

6. Advertising choices

  • Ad personalization: where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we present a consent prompt before showing personalized ads. You may be able to change your choice later through the App.
  • Device controls: on iOS, you can control tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Remove ads: subscribing to Flag Scan Pro removes advertising from the App.

Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads; the ads you see will simply be less relevant.

7. Data retention

  • Account data and scan references: kept while your account is active. When you request deletion, we delete or de-identify them within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.
  • Subscription records: RevenueCat, Apple, and Google may keep purchase records for as long as their own policies and legal obligations require.
  • On-device data: kept until you sign out, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its own policies.
